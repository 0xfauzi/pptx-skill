# consulting_slide_composition v1

General craft for composing a consulting slide. Concrete values - fonts,
colors, sizes, motifs - belong to the StyleGuide, never here. Where this
skill and the reference's observed practice conflict, the reference wins.

## Principles

The action title carries the argument. It is a full sentence stating the
so-what - subject, verb, operative figure - and the body exists to prove it.
A subordinate second deck qualifies scope or method, never a second claim.
The test runs both ways: the title asserts on its own, and the body demands
exactly that title.

Vertical logic makes the page read downward as one proof. Under the title
sit the elements that prove it; below those, qualifications and
implications. Every block must complete the sentence "the title holds
because". A block that cannot is on the wrong page.

Trackers and agendas locate the reader. Dividers repeat the full agenda with
the live item accented and the rest muted; a tracker or kicker names the
live section on every content page.

Framework exhibits turn judgment into geometry and earn their place only
when the geometry does work. A 2x2 positions entities on two named axes and
pays off in the quadrants - label each quadrant with its implication, not
its coordinates. Harvey-ball comparisons rank options in rows against
criteria in columns, fill degree as score; order rows so the recommended
option reads first or carries the accent. A heatmap matrix shades cells by
intensity so the pattern - the hot column, the empty quadrant - is the
message; if no pattern shows, it is a table wearing color. MECE columns run
in parallel, each headed by its own mini-claim over a hairline rule,
non-overlapping and jointly exhaustive, so the claims together restate the
title.

Annotation is the register's density. Where a banking page lets the
exhibit's numerals speak, a consulting page argues alongside its exhibit:
numbered anchors key prose to the marks they explain, a boxed takeaway
restates the implication, footnotes carry method and assumptions, the
source line carries provenance. Emphasis follows: banking accents the
concluded figure; consulting accents the step being proven.

## Worked example: benchmark findings page

type.kicker names the live section, echoing the tracker. type.title asserts
that field productivity trails every benchmarked peer. Three MECE columns -
volume, coverage, follow-through - each open with a type.h2 mini-claim over
a color.rule hairline and prove it with type.data_label figures; the
lagging figure carries color.negative and type.emphasis, and color.accent1
anchor markers key each column to its type.body callout. A full-width
takeaway band restates the implication in type.emphasis; type.footnote
carries the benchmark method; type.source_line closes the page.

## Worked example: agenda divider

The agenda repeats down one shared left edge at space.lg intervals: the
live item in color.ink beside the page's only color.accent1 marker,
completed and upcoming items in color.ink_muted. No exhibit competes - the
divider's whole job is location.

## Checklist

- Does the title state the so-what, and does every block below prove it?
- Does each framework exhibit's geometry do work its prose could not?
- Are column panels mutually exclusive and jointly exhaustive?
- Can the reader name the live section from the page alone?
- Do anchors, takeaway, footnotes, and source line each do their one job?

## Anti-patterns

- A label title over a page of evidence, the so-what left unsaid.
- A 2x2 with unlabeled quadrants: coordinates without implications.
- Harvey balls ranking nothing: rows unordered, no accented recommendation.
- Column panels that overlap, or leave part of the title unproven.
- Annotation without anchoring: callouts the eye cannot tie to a mark.
