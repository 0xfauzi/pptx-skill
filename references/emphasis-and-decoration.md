# Emphasis, fills and marks: questions, and what decks measurably do

Questions to ask about every mark on a page, with what professional decks measurably do;
never rules. The guide's motifs, fill roles and `exhibit_habits.emphasis_devices` decide.
Counts are pages carrying each device, of 578 coded content pages (bank 231, consulting
317, chart book 30), one coder per page, good to about plus or minus 2 to 3.

## Questions per mark

- What is this mark's job: separate, select, locate, connect? Can you name it in one
  phrase? A mark with no job is decoration.
- Which device does this house use for that job (spacing, a hairline, a heavier rule, a
  fill, a box)? Use the house's, at every recurrence, the same way.
- What is the scarce device in this house: one emphasis colour on one item (88 pages), not
  every fill. What is it pointing at, and is that the claim?
- Does every connector, bracket and arrow visibly join the two things it relates?
- Does the house show gradients, shadows, rounded corners, icons? If not, neither should
  this page.
- Can the absence of a mark carry meaning (one panel left unboxed to prove "except this
  one")?

## Fills are role-coded and often several

Shaded key rows on 52 pages, dark label cells and banners on 37, takeaway bands on 46, grey
sidebars on 11 of 29 pages of one deck. Fills in these decks are coded by role (header,
total, label, takeaway, sidebar) and several appear on one page. *Inferred*: what the
evidence supports as scarce is one emphasis colour on one item, not one fill per page.

## Big numbers: of what, and for whom?

Big numbers are rare: 0 bank and chart-book pages, 7 consulting pages; where used,
consulting pages paired them with icons or rings, and the craft was pairing each number
with its base and population. Ask what each number is a share of, and for whom, and put
that on the page.

## Motifs recur identically

A device the house uses (a kicker over a hairline, a numbered badge, a header treatment)
teaches the reader a grammar; repeat it identically at every recurrence. Deliberate
repetition or cross-page consistency was recorded on 82 pages.
