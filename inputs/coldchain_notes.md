cold chain market sizing - rough notes (from calls + desk research, all numbers rough, treat as illustrative)

- US temp-controlled warehousing capacity ~ 4.1bn cubic feet 2025, growing ~4% pa
- pharma cold chain (2-8C + frozen) is the fast segment, ~11% pa, biologics + GLP-1 driving
- food is still 70% of volume but low growth ~2-3%
- last mile refrigerated delivery: fragmented, top 5 players ~ 18% share
- avg utilisation of public refrigerated warehouses ~ 82%, tight in southeast + texas
- build cost new cold storage ~ $220-260 per sq ft vs ~$120 ambient
- energy is ~25-30% of opex, big lever (ammonia/CO2 systems, solar, thermal storage)
- three ways in: (1) buy a regional 3PL (2) greenfield pharma-grade sites near hubs (3) tech-enabled last mile
- rough TAM: US cold chain logistics ~ $95bn 2025 -> ~ $135bn 2030 ; pharma slice ~$21bn -> ~$35bn
- client = mid-size freight forwarder, ~$1.2bn revenue, no cold capability today
- question from CEO: is it worth it and where do we play
